FAQ

Where are my pictures?

Every picture stays in DCIM on your iPhone. Save one to put it in your Photos library too.

DCIM can't save to Photos

iPhone → Settings → Apps → DCIM → Photos, and allow DCIM to add photos.

What does DCIM Pro include?

Every camera, and every new camera we add: for good with Lifetime, or for a year with the yearly plan. You can also buy single cameras on their own. Purchases are shared with your Family Sharing group.

I bought DCIM Pro on another device

Open the DCIM Pro screen and tap “Restore Purchases”.

How do I cancel the yearly plan?

iPhone → Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → DCIM.

Is my data private?

Yes — DCIM collects no data. See our Privacy Policy.
