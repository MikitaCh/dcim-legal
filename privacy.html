Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

DCIM is a camera app. Your pictures and everything you do in it stay on your device. We don't collect any data.

Camera and microphone

DCIM uses the camera to take pictures and clips, and the microphone to record sound for clips. Pictures and clips are processed on your iPhone and kept in the app on your device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Photos

When you save a picture, DCIM adds it to your Photos library, with add-only access: the app can't read your library. When you import photos to develop them again, you pick them in Apple's photo picker, and DCIM only receives the photos you pick.

Purchases

Purchases and the yearly subscription are processed by Apple. DCIM never sees your payment details. To keep your purchases and the count of free pictures in step across your devices, DCIM uses your own iCloud (Apple's key-value storage), which only you and Apple can access.

No tracking

DCIM contains no ads, no third-party trackers and no analytics SDKs. It has no account and no servers of its own. Nothing is sold or shared.

Children

DCIM is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them.
