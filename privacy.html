Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

DCIM is a camera app. Your pictures and everything you do in it stay on your device. The only data that leaves it is a record of your in-app purchases, described below.

Camera and microphone

DCIM uses the camera to take pictures and clips, and the microphone to record sound for clips. Pictures and clips are processed on your iPhone and kept in the app on your device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Photos

When you save a picture, DCIM adds it to your Photos library, with add-only access: the app can't read your library. When you import photos to develop them again, you pick them in Apple's photo picker, and DCIM only receives the photos you pick.

Purchases

Purchases and the yearly subscription are processed by Apple. DCIM never sees your payment details. To keep your purchases and the count of free pictures in step across your devices, DCIM uses your own iCloud (Apple's key-value storage), which only you and Apple can access.

To understand how DCIM Pro is doing (revenue, refunds, renewals), DCIM sends a record of your in-app purchases to RevenueCat, a service that processes purchase data for app developers. With the purchase record RevenueCat receives the device's vendor identifier (IDFV, an identifier Apple gives each app developer, not the advertising identifier), the device model, the iOS version, the device's language and your App Store country, and it sees the IP address the request comes from. The record is linked only to a random identifier the app creates, not to your name, email or Apple Account. None of this is used for advertising or tracking. RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

No tracking

DCIM contains no ads and no third-party trackers, and doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites. It has no account and no servers of its own. Nothing is sold.

Children

DCIM is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them.
